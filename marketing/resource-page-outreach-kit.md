# Resource-Page Outreach Kit

**The channel:** TAFEs, RTOs, community legal centres and small-business advisory pages
maintain "useful links" pages as routine housekeeping — and they link practical, free,
no-paywall references without needing an editor's yes. The cheat sheets and guides are
built for exactly this. Target: **10 emails a week, 20 minutes total.**

**Why it works when media doesn't reply:** no news cycle, no gatekeeper, no timing rules.
A course coordinator who finds the cheat sheet useful just adds it. Every link is
Australian, educational and topical — precisely the profile that fixes the
AI-directory-heavy backlink history.

**Rules:** never pay for a link · one follow-up max · log every send in the table below ·
always link the cheat sheet or a guide, never the homepage or /app.

---

## Finding targets — paste these into Google (10–15 minutes finds a week's batch)

**Hospitality / cookery training:**
- `"useful links" OR "resources" hospitality site:tafe.edu.au`
- `"useful links" fair work hospitality course`
- `RSA course "student resources" links`
- `certificate III commercial cookery "resources" site:.edu.au`

**Childcare / early education (your best-converting audience):**
- `"useful links" OR "resources" early childhood education site:.edu.au`
- `certificate III early childhood "student resources"`
- `family day care scheme "resources for educators"`

**NDIS / community services:**
- `NDIS provider "useful links" OR "resources" employment site:.org.au`
- `disability support worker course "resources"`

**Small business / legal:**
- `"helpful links" small business site:.org.au`
- `community legal centre "employment law" "links"`
- `small business advisory "useful resources" fair work`
- `bookkeepers association "member resources" payroll`

**What qualifies a page:** it already links external resources (especially fairwork.gov.au),
it's Australian, and there's a named contact or generic email somewhere on the site. Skip
pages that look abandoned (dead links, "last updated 2021").

heading into venues / educators / new providers] tend to need exactly this. If it's
useful for your page, we'd be glad to see it listed. There's also a fuller guide at
fitzhr.com/[hospitality]-award-guide if you'd rather link something deeper.

Cheers,
Blake Fitzgerald
Founder | Fitz HR
+61 400 211 014 · blake@fitzhr.com · FitzHR.com

**Cheat-sheet URL per audience:** hospitality · restaurant · retail · manufacturing ·
schads · health · childrens — all follow `fitzhr.com/{name}-award-cheat-sheet`.
Match the sheet to the audience: TAFE cookery → hospitality or restaurant; early
childhood RTO → childrens; NDIS org → schads.
