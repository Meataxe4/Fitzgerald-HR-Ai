## Caption (post body)

**Your next employee complaint might be written by ChatGPT. 👀**

It's a new reality for small business owners: staff are using AI to draft complaints and grievances that are longer, more formal and more legal-sounding than ever. Ten pages of citations and confident legal language — and no HR team to help you make sense of it.

Here's the catch: AI is great at *tone*, not *truth*. It polishes the wording, but it doesn't check the facts or apply employment law. A complaint can look airtight and still be legally shaky.

So what actually protects you? Not panicking at the formatting — responding to the **facts** underneath it, and following a **proper, consistent process**. Handled well, an AI-polished complaint holds no fear. Handled badly, it becomes a real dispute (and a real cost).

That's exactly what **Fitz HR** is built for: small businesses that don't have an HR team but still have to get employee complaints and disputes right. Instant AI answers, backed by **real HR experts** — so you respond the right way, every time.
