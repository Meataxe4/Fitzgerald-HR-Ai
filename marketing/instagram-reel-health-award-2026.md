## What the reel says (all sourced from PR814029)
1. **New structure** — Health Professional pay now follows **AQF levels (5, 6, 7) by years of experience**; the old Level‑1 pay‑point / Level‑2–4 structure is replaced.
2. **Why** — minimum rates move up as part of Fair Work's **gender‑based undervaluation** priority review.
3. **When & who** — applies from the **first full pay period on or after 1 October 2026**; existing employees are protected by **transitional rates** (Schedule J) so no one goes backwards.
4. **CTA** — re‑map roles to AQF levels and update payroll → fitzhr.com.

## Caption (post body)

**📢 Health Award change alert — MA000027.**

From your first full pay period on or after **1 October 2026**, the Health Professionals & Support Services Award moves to a new **AQF‑based pay structure** and minimum rates lift — part of Fair Work's **gender‑based undervaluation** review.

Here's the short version 👇
• Health Professional pay now follows **AQF levels 5, 6 & 7** by years of experience (the old pay‑point levels are gone)
• **Minimum rates move up**
• Applies from your **first full pay period on/after 1 Oct 2026**
• **Existing staff are protected** by transitional rates — no one goes backwards

What to do: re‑map your roles to the new AQF classifications and update payroll before your October pay run.
