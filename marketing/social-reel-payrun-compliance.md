## Caption (post body)

**Your pay run went through. That doesn't mean it was compliant. 👀**

A pay run can land perfectly in every account and still be hiding real risk. Behind a "clean" run there's often an overstretched payroll team applying manual workarounds to interpret one of the most complex award systems in the world. Four red flags to watch for:

1️⃣ Systems that aren't set up compliantly (think rounding rules quietly shaving worked time)
2️⃣ Manual workarounds & undocumented overrides to get the run out on time
3️⃣ Payroll know-how walking out the door when one key person leaves
4️⃣ Weak payroll governance — decisions sitting in one person's head

And the stakes are enormous. Across the last six financial years, cumulative back-payments owed to Australian workers are close to **$2.5 billion**, according to the Fair Work Ombudsman.

The lesson: compliance can't rely on the system alone. As the experts put it — good payroll compliance doesn't end where the roster ends. It's people, process AND technology.

That's where **Fitz HR** helps small teams: award-smart AI backed by real HR experts, checking rates, penalties and allowances — so your compliance isn't trapped in one overstretched person's head.
