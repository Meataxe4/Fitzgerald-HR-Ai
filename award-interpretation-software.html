Quick answer

Award interpretation software applies the rules of an Australian modern award — classifications, minimum rates, penalty rates, shift loadings, allowances, casual loading and overtime — so employers pay and manage staff correctly. It comes in two kinds: payroll-side tools that calculate each timesheet (rostering and payroll platforms), and decision-side tools like Fitz HR that answer award questions in plain English, flag roster breaches before they happen, and generate award-correct documents. Why Interpretation Errors Are Expensive

Award misapplication compounds silently: a flat evening loading applied as a percentage, a casual loading stacked on a penalty that already includes it, a minimum engagement missed across a year of rosters. Fair Work penalties for award non-compliance reach $93,900 per contravention for companies, and the process failures that follow — undocumented warnings, wrong termination steps — feed unfair dismissal claims that typically cost $10,000–$50,000+. Frequently Asked Questions

What is award interpretation software?
Software that applies the rules of an Australian modern award — classifications, minimum rates, penalties, loadings, allowances and overtime — so employers pay and manage staff correctly. Payroll-side tools calculate timesheets; decision-side tools like Fitz HR answer questions, check rosters before publishing and generate award-correct documents.
What software flags split-shift and minimum-engagement breaches?
Fitz HR’s Roster Stress Tester checks a roster against your modern award — including split-shift allowances and minimum engagement rules under awards like the Restaurant Award MA000119 and Hospitality Award MA000009 — and flags breaches before the roster is published.
Do I still need this if my payroll does award interpretation?
Payroll-side interpretation works after the fact — it calculates pay for hours already worked. It doesn’t answer questions before decisions are made, check a roster before it goes out, or produce compliant documents. Most employers run both layers together.
