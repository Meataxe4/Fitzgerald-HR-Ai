About Fitz HR

Award-aware HR software for Australian small business

Fitz HR is award-aware, AI-powered HR compliance software for Australian small business. It is not payroll or rostering software: it sits alongside Xero, MYOB, Deputy and Tanda as the award and compliance layer. It helps owner-operators in hospitality, retail, manufacturing, community services (SCHADS/NDIS), health and children's services stay compliant with the Fair Work Act 2009 (Cth) and their specific modern award with instant guidance, compliant documents and on-demand access to qualified HR professionals, 24/7. For businesses without a dedicated HR team, it replaces the need for an external HR consultant for all but the most complex legal proceedings.

HR doesn’t fail at 9am in a meeting room. It fails off-hours: a chef walks out mid-service, a support worker no-shows a 2am shift, a theft allegation lands on a Sunday, and a manager makes a call without the right information. No documented warning, no fair process, no paper trail. Fitz HR exists for that moment, and every routine compliance decision before it.

Built by operators and HR professionals with real-world experience in Australian Fair Work compliance, workplace disputes and award interpretation, for the businesses that can’t afford to get it wrong.

What Fitz HR is

The compliance layer for award-covered businesses

How it fits

Fitz HR operates within the Australian employment law framework, with guidance aligned to the Fair Work Act 2009 (Cth), the National Employment Standards (NES) and seven modern awards: Hospitality MA000009, Restaurant MA000119, General Retail MA000004, Manufacturing MA000010, SCHADS MA000100, Health Professionals MA000027 and Children’s Services MA000120. It is not payroll or rostering software; it works alongside platforms including Xero, MYOB, Deputy and Tanda as the HR compliance layer.

Who it’s for

Australian small businesses in award-heavy, shift-based frontline industries: venues, shops, workshops, care services, clinics and childcare centres, single-site or multi-site. It suits owner-operators and managers responsible for staff compliance, rostering decisions and employee issues without a dedicated internal HR team.

Common use cases

Handling underperformance and disciplinary action, managing unfair dismissal risk, interpreting award penalty rates and allowances, responding to Fair Work complaints and staff grievances, and generating compliant employment documents before a situation escalates.

Common questions

What is Fitz HR?

Fitz HR is an award-aware, AI-powered HR compliance platform for Australian small business. It provides instant guidance grounded in your specific modern award (seven awards across hospitality, retail, manufacturing, community services (SCHADS/NDIS), health and children’s services) plus the Fair Work Act 2009, generates compliant employment documents, and connects operators with qualified HR professionals for complex disputes and crisis situations. Your award, not generic HR.

What awards does Fitz HR cover?

Seven modern awards: Hospitality (MA000009), Restaurant (MA000119), General Retail (MA000004), Manufacturing (MA000010), SCHADS (MA000100), Health Professionals (MA000027) and Children’s Services (MA000120). Every AI response is aligned to your award’s classification levels, penalty rates, loadings and overtime provisions. For example, the Hospitality Award’s full penalty schedule (Saturday 125%/150%, Sunday 150%/175%, public holiday 225%/250%, full-time or part-time vs casual), evening loadings ($2.95/hr) and night loadings ($4.42/hr). See the complete 2026 Hospitality Award rates guide.

Is Fitz HR a replacement for an HR consultant?

For most small businesses, yes. Fitz HR combines instant AI guidance for routine HR questions with direct access to qualified HR professionals for complex investigations, disputes and crisis situations. The only area Fitz HR does not cover is formal legal representation at the Fair Work Commission, which requires an employment lawyer. See the full Fitz HR vs HR consultants comparison.

How much does Fitz HR cost?

Fitz HR starts at $0/month forever, with 20 AI chats a month. Paid plans start at $249/year (Starter) and $449/year (Pro), and include unlimited AI chat. No lock-in contracts, no termination fees, 14-day money-back guarantee. Full pricing is published at fitzhr.com/pricing, with no sales call required to see it.

What HR documents can Fitz HR generate?

Warning letters, PIPs, employment contracts, termination letters, probation review documents, position descriptions, onboarding documents and more, all aligned to your modern award and the Fair Work Act.
