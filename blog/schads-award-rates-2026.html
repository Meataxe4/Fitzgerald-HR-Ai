SCHADS Award Rates 2026 — Pay & Penalty Rates

1 Jul 2026 Updated 1 Jul 2026 By Fitz HR 6 min read Reviewed against FWO Pay Guide MA000100

The Social, Community, Home Care and Disability Services Industry Award MA000100 covers a huge, NDIS-heavy workforce across several streams — and its pay-point structure, sleepovers and broken shifts make it one of the trickiest awards to run. This guide sets out the 2026 rates.

Under the SCHADS Award MA000100 (2026), a Social & Community Services Level 1 pay point 1 employee earns $27.55/hr full-time. Saturday is 150% (175% casual), Sunday 200% (225% casual) and public holidays 250% (275% casual). Afternoon shifts attract 12.5% and night shifts 15%. Sleepovers, broken shifts and on-call are paid as separate allowances on top.

Rates current as at 2026-07-01 (Annual Wage Review), sourced from the Fair Work Ombudsman Pay Guide MA000100. Next review 2027-07-01.

Quick answer

Under the SCHADS Award (MA000100), the minimum full-time adult rate from 1 July 2026 starts at $27.55/hour (Social and community services - Level 1 - pay point 1 (social community services)). Saturdays pay 150% (full/part-time) or 175% (casual); Sundays 200% (full/part-time) or 225% (casual); public holidays 250% (full/part-time) or 275% (casual). Sleepovers, Broken Shifts & Shift Loadings

Afternoon shifts attract 12.5% and night shifts 15%. A sleepover attracts an allowance of $62.87 per night on top of any active work, and a broken shift attracts $21.81 (one unpaid break) or $28.87 (two). See our sleepover guide and broken shift guide.

Common Mistakes That Trigger Audits

Using the wrong stream or pay point. SACS, home care and other streams have different rates — the pay point matters.
Absorbing sleepovers into the hourly rate. A sleepover is a separate $62.87 allowance on top of any active work.
Missing the broken-shift allowance. A broken shift attracts $21.81 or $28.87 depending on the number of unpaid breaks.
Applying the wrong minimum engagement. SACS is 3 hours; other streams and casuals can be 2 — see our rostering notes.
