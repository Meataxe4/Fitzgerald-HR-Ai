Health Professionals Award Allowances Guide

1 Jul 2026 Updated 1 Jul 2026 By Fitz HR 4 min read Reviewed against FWO Pay Guide MA000027

Allowances in private health are easy to overlook — uniforms, tools, first aid and more. The Health Professionals Award MA000027 requires them to be paid when triggered. Here are the common ones for 2026.

Under MA000027, common allowances include a uniform allowance of $1.26 per shift where the employer requires a uniform but does not launder it, and a range of role-specific allowances. Allowances are paid on top of the base rate whenever the trigger condition is met — not absorbed into the hourly rate or a salary unless the arrangement expressly and adequately covers them.

How Allowances Interact with Salaries

An annualised salary only "covers" allowances if the written arrangement expressly absorbs them and the salary demonstrably exceeds base plus every triggered allowance. Otherwise, allowances must be paid separately — see our annualised wage guide.

Common Mistakes That Trigger Audits

Assuming a salary covers allowances. Only if expressly absorbed and adequately funded — otherwise pay them separately.
Forgetting the uniform allowance. Where a uniform is required but not laundered by the employer, the allowance applies.
Missing first-aid allowances. Employees who hold and use a first-aid qualification at the employer’s request are owed the allowance.
