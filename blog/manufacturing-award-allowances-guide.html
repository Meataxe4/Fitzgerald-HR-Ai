Manufacturing Award Allowances Guide

1 Jul 2026 Updated 1 Jul 2026 By Fitz HR 4 min read Reviewed against FWO Pay Guide MA000010

Allowances are easy to forget and add up fast. The Manufacturing Award MA000010 includes a range of allowances — tool, meal, disability and more — that must be paid when the trigger conditions are met. Here are the common ones for 2026.

Under MA000010, common allowances include a tool allowance of $0.47 per hour for tradespeople who provide their own tools, and a meal allowance of $19.14 per meal when an employee works qualifying overtime. Allowances are paid on top of the base rate whenever the trigger condition is met — not absorbed into it.

Rates current as at 2026-07-01 (Annual Wage Review), sourced from the Fair Work Ombudsman Pay Guide MA000010. Next review 2027-07-01.

Common Allowances — Manufacturing

Tool allowance: $0.47 per hour (tradesperson supplying own tools)
Meal allowance: $19.14 per meal (qualifying overtime)
Plus disability, leading-hand and first-aid allowances where they apply

How Allowances Work

Some allowances are per-hour (like the tool allowance), some are per-occasion (like the meal allowance), and some are weekly. A flat wage does not "cover" allowances unless it demonstrably exceeds the base rate plus every allowance actually triggered — most flat arrangements do not. When in doubt, pay the allowance separately and keep the record.

Common Mistakes That Trigger Audits

Assuming a flat rate covers allowances. It only complies if it exceeds base plus every triggered allowance — most do not.
Forgetting the tool allowance. Tradespeople who supply their own tools are owed the per-hour tool allowance.
Missing the overtime meal allowance. Qualifying overtime triggers a meal allowance per meal.
