Health Professionals Award Rates 2026 — Pay & Penalties

1 Jul 2026 Updated 1 Jul 2026 By Fitz HR 6 min read Reviewed against FWO Pay Guide MA000027

The Health Professionals and Support Services Award MA000027 covers a wide private-sector workforce — allied health, dental assistants, pathology collectors and administration — across several streams. This guide sets out the 2026 rates and the rules behind them.

Under the Health Professionals Award MA000027 (2026), a Support Services Level 1 employee earns $26.97/hr full-time. Saturday and Sunday are both 150% (175% casual) and public holidays 250% (275% casual). A 15% shift loading applies to certain Monday–Friday shifts. The casual minimum engagement is 3 hours, and the casual loading of 25% is already built into the casual penalty percentages.

Rates current as at 2026-10-01 (FWC determination PR814029 (health professional restructure, first full pay period on or after 1 October 2026); support services, dental assistant and pathology collector rates per the 1 July 2026 Annual Wage Review), sourced from the Fair Work Ombudsman Pay Guide MA000027. Rates by Stream & Level

MA000027 splits into streams — support services, dental assistants, pathology collectors and health professionals — each with its own levels. From 1 October 2026 the health professional stream has no pay points: Level 1 is set by the AQF level of the profession's minimum qualification (Schedule B) and years of experience in the profession, and Levels 2.1, 2.2, 3 and 4 are defined by role. Staff employed on 30 September 2026 keep their previous rate where it is higher (clause J.4.3). See our guide to the 1 October 2026 changes. Health-professional rates run well above support roles, so the stream and level both matter.

Penalty Rates & Shift Loading

Weekend and public holiday penalties are a percentage of the minimum hourly rate; casual rates are all-inclusive of the 25% loading:

When workedFull-time / part-timeCasual (all-inclusive)
Saturday150%175%
Sunday150%175%
Public holiday250%275%

A 15% shift loading applies to defined Monday–Friday shifts. Overtime is 150% for the first 2 hours and 200% after.

Casual Loading & Minimum Engagement

Casual employees receive a 25% loading and a minimum engagement of 3 hours per shift. Many practices also use annualised wage arrangements for full-time professionals — see our annualised wage guide for the strict conditions.

Common Mistakes That Trigger Audits

✗
Using the wrong stream. A dental assistant, pathology collector and health professional sit in different streams with different rates.
✗
Still using pay points for health professionals. They no longer exist from 1 October 2026 — classify Level 1 by AQF level and years of experience, and check the retained-rate rule for anyone employed on 30 September 2026.
✗
Treating a salary as covering everything. Annualised wages have strict conditions and a mandatory reconciliation — see our annualised wage guide.
✗
Rostering casuals under 3 hours. The casual minimum engagement is 3 hours.
✗
Paying Sunday rates on public holidays. Public holidays are 250%/275%.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the base rate for a health support worker in 2026?
A Support Services Level 1 employee earns $26.97/hr full-time under MA000027. Health professionals (physiotherapy, etc.) are paid by AQF level and years of experience at Level 1, and by role at Levels 2.1, 2.2, 3 and 4 — from $32.44/hr to $65.77/hr full-time from 1 October 2026.
Do health professionals still have pay points?
No — pay points were removed from the health professional stream from the first full pay period on or after 1 October 2026 (FWC determination PR814029). Level 1 is now AQF level x years of experience; Levels 2.1, 2.2, 3 and 4 are single rates by role. Support services, dental assistant and pathology collector levels are unchanged.
What are the weekend penalty rates under MA000027?
Saturday and Sunday are both 150% (175% casual); public holidays are 250% (275% casual).
What is the casual minimum engagement under the Health Professionals Award?
3 hours per shift. A casual must be paid for at least 3 hours each time they attend work.
Is there a shift loading under MA000027?
Yes — a 15% loading applies to defined Monday–Friday shifts. Overtime is 150% for the first 2 hours and 200% after.
