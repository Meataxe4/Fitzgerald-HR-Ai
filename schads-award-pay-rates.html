Quick answer

Under the SCHADS Award (MA000100), the minimum full-time adult rate starts at $27.55/hour (social and community services Level 1, pay point 1), with Level 2 pay point 1 at $36.22/hour and Level 3 pay point 1 at $40.49/hour. Saturdays pay 150% (full/part-time) or 175% (casual); Sundays 200% (full/part-time) or 225% (casual); public holidays 250% (full/part-time) or 275% (casual). Casual percentages already include the 25% casual loading. Christmas, New Year & Public Holiday Rates (2026–27)

The summer public holidays are where SCHADS Award payroll errors cluster, because the public holiday penalty is the largest multiplier in the award and Boxing Day falls on a Saturday this year, which brings substitute-day rules into play. For a social and community services Level 1 employee on the current minimum of $27.55 an hour:

Hour workedFull-time / part-timeCasual
Ordinary Monday–Friday hour$27.55$34.44
Public holiday hour (250% / 275% casual)$68.88$75.76

The same multiplier applies at every classification level, so take the level’s ordinary rate from the table above and multiply. The casual public holiday percentage already includes the 25% casual loading; do not add it again. The national public holidays in the period are:

  • Christmas Day — Friday 25 December 2026
  • Boxing Day — Saturday 26 December 2026 (most states and territories gazette an additional public holiday on Monday 28 December)
  • New Year's Day — Friday 1 January 2027
  • Australia Day — Tuesday 26 January 2027

Each state and territory gazettes its own list, including part-day holidays such as Christmas Eve and New Year’s Eve in some jurisdictions, so check your state’s list before the roster is published. The penalty applies to hours worked on the gazetted day; where a substitute day is declared, the award sets out which day attracts the penalty, and that is a question worth asking Fitz before the pay run rather than after.

What Changed in 2026, and What Changes Next

The rates on this page took effect from the first full pay period on or after 1 July 2026, following the Fair Work Commission’s 4.75% Annual Wage Review increase (see what the 2026 increase means for the SCHADS Award), and apply for the 2026–27 financial year. The next Annual Wage Review decision is expected in June 2027 and takes effect from the first full pay period on or after 1 July 2027. Superannuation guarantee is 12%. Exception: home care (disability) employees under Schedule E receive an interim increase of about 15% from the first full pay period on or after 1 December 2026; the home care rates on this page are the 1 July 2026 rates until they are updated.
