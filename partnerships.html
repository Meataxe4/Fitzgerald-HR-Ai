PaySauce

PaySauce started with a simple idea. Small business owners have enough on their plate. Payroll should not be the thing that keeps them up at night.

PaySauce is payroll for small Australian businesses. Employee details, timesheets and leave sit alongside the pay run, award interpretation helps calculate the applicable rates, penalties and allowances, and PAYG, super, STP reporting and payslips are handled as part of payday. You can run it from your phone.

How the two fit together: Fitz HR works out what your award requires, documents the decision and keeps the record. PaySauce takes it from there on payday. Read So, you’ve hired someone. Now what? for the full picture.

Fitz HR customers: mention Fitz HR when you sign up to PaySauce and they will set you up for free and walk you through it with a tutorial.
